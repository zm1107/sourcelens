Style, fingerprints, prices — three angles, one verdict

A single angle can be evaded; three angles look at the same bid at once — each is an independent line of evidence, and every point of the composite score is explainable.

Three-part composite score

Fingerprint evidence, writing style and price patterns merge into one composite score, graded high / medium / low risk.

  • Explainable — every point maps to visible evidence; no black-box numbers
  • Adjustable thresholds — risk cut-offs follow your review standards and re-grade instantly
  • Division of labor — the machine surfaces suspicious pairs; the final call stays human

Bid-price pattern analysis

Extracts amounts from quoted price lists, flags suspicious regularities across a set of bids, and contrasts them as bar charts.

  • One glance — side-by-side price bars show which bids move in lockstep
  • Feeds the score — pattern hits enter the composite score with per-pair details
  • False-positive guards — normal pricing is not easily misjudged

Writing-style profile

Like handwriting analysis — word choice, sentence length, punctuation, boilerplate, quantity phrasing, part-of-speech habits and co-occurring typos blend into a style-similarity score.

  • Co-occurring typos are the strongest signal — the same mistake betrays the same hands
  • Nearly a thousand domain typo pairs — exact dictionary matching only, no guessing
  • Two-key review — accept / exclude typos in batches; exclusions are remembered

Document fingerprint forensics

Like physical evidence — 14 independent channels inspect the traces "beyond the text": metadata, images, editing lineage, hidden content…

  • Shared-entity locks — matching phones, emails, ID numbers or accounts become high-grade evidence
  • Hard to erase — structure trees, approximate fingerprints and image attributes survive rewording
  • Page-level citations — every finding can be checked against the source, graded hard / strong / soft

Five steps from a stack of bids to a forensic report

Mix PDF / DOCX / DOC freely; scanned pages are OCR-ed automatically and offline. Re-analysis of the same batch finishes in seconds.

Not all evidence is equally reliable

SourceLens grades evidence by how hard it is to tamper with, so your attention lands where it matters.

Hard evidence Near self-proving signals of common origin — extremely hard to scrub even deliberately; a single item can raise the risk grade sharply
Strong evidence Substantive findings corroborated across channels — the backbone of the assessment
Soft evidence Environmental corroboration only — explicitly labeled "alterable, forgeable; verify before trusting"
Honesty clause: all conclusions are decision aids; final determinations require human review of the source documents. Missing or wiped metadata does not imply different origin. Use only within lawful authorization (bid supervision, forensic investigation, internal compliance review).
